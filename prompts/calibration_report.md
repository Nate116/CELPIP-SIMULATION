## 2. Benchmark Exemplars & Calibrated Score Profiles

### Writing Task 1 Exemplar Comparison
- **CLB 6 Response**:
  *"Dear manager, I write for you to tell you that water is leaking in my kitchen. It start yesterday night and make big puddle. I am very sad and angry please fix fast. You can call my cell phone. Thank you, Alex."*
  - *Diagnostics*: 48 words (severe task undercount), elementary vocabulary, repetitive subject-verb order, informal tone in formal tenancy complaint. **Score: CELPIP 5-6**.

- **CLB 9 Response**:
  *"Dear Mr. Peterson, I am writing to formally notify you regarding a plumbing leak that has manifested under the kitchen sink in unit 402. The leak commenced late yesterday evening and has begun causing water accumulation beneath the cabinetry. As this poses potential structural damage and significant inconvenience, I kindly request that an emergency plumber be dispatched as soon as possible. Furthermore, I would appreciate confirmation of an inspection window so I can make myself available. You can reach me directly at 555-0192. Respectfully, Jordan Smith."*
  - *Diagnostics*: 95 words (approaching target), clear formal register, correct collocations, varied subordinate clauses, all 3 points addressed. **Score: CELPIP 9-10**.

- **CLB 12 Response**:
  *"Dear Strata Council and Building Management, I am writing to apprise you of an urgent maintenance concern within suite 304 that necessitates immediate intervention. Late yesterday evening, a persistent pipe failure was detected beneath the main kitchen plumbing assembly, resulting in significant water seepage that threatens both the cabinetry and adjacent laminate flooring. To mitigate the risk of escalating water damage to neighboring suites, I urge management to dispatch a certified plumbing specialist first thing tomorrow morning. Additionally, please clarify whether temporary moisture containment equipment can be provided in the interim, as well as the procedure for logging an insurance claim if subsurface deterioration is found. I will be working remotely tomorrow to facilitate prompt contractor access. I remain accessible at 604-555-0184 should further details be required. Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter. Sincerely, Morgan Vance."*
  - *Diagnostics*: 156 words (optimal 150-200 band), sophisticated Canadian vocabulary (*apprise, necessitates immediate intervention, mitigate the risk, laminate flooring*), perfect register control, flawless cohesion and task fulfillment. **Score: CELPIP 12**.
